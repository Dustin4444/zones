## Workload and measurement boundary

One central Deel treasury paid 1,000 distinct deterministic devnet users over a 60-second payout
window. Every user then waited a seeded independent interval before each action:

| Action | Scheduled interval |
|---|---:|
| Payout | 0–60s |
| Earn after payout | 1–60s |
| Redeem after Earn | 5–300s |
| Offramp after redeem | 1–60s |

Concurrency was bounded at 16 L1 sends, 32 Zone sends, 16 private-RPC authorizations, and 32 RPC
reads. Each user also received a 1 PATHUSD Zone fee reserve. Admission required the reserve for the
transaction's maximum liability even though the final Zone effective gas price and actual charge
were zero.

Latency is protocol/service E2E from the runner's local action-intent boundary through the terminal
private Zone or public L1 RPC observation. It includes preparation, queueing, submission,
settlement, returned-deposit ingestion, and the final RPC read. It does **not** include a real Privy
HTTP request, Privy networking, HSM, or remote signing latency.

Every Earn, redeem, and offramp `processWithdrawals` receipt contained one withdrawal, so none of
that cost was amortized in this run. Earn and redeem callback execution and their internal returned
`depositEncrypted` call are already inside the full receipt; there is no second L1 deposit receipt
to add. Returned-deposit Zone ingestion and Zone `finalizeWithdrawalBatch` are unmetered system
transactions (`gasUsed = 0`, effective gas price 0). The subsequent return-state `submitBatch` is
separately included above. Shared `submitBatch` receipts are allocated only across their external
workload inputs; the zero-address `finalizeWithdrawalBatch` system transaction is excluded from
the denominator.

The audited live `submitBatch` call used empty `verifierConfig` and empty `proof`, with a sequencer
signature. These are therefore **batch-submission/attestation costs**, not production validity-proof
costs. One decoded example is transaction
`0x28b2a74f3581c038f6d3ba60e1d95b3c2bb653e22e7ee367b4b9ab87af79aa64`.

## Realism limits

- AlphaUSD is the deployed DLUSD analogue; this was not literal DLUSD.
- `Tempo Earn Local Vault` is a `Simple4626` fixture, not the production metavault.
- The protocol/queue/settlement boundary is live, but Privy HTTP, network, and HSM latency are absent.
- The current proof bytes are empty, so this does not price production validity proving.
